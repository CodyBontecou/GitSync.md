# GitSync.md pricing

Canonical product page: https://gitsync.md/
Reviewed: October 26, 2026.

## iPhone and iPad

GitSync.md costs $19.99 once on the US App Store. The purchase includes unlimited repositories, the editor, manual Git tools, and optional Background Sync. There is no subscription. Local store prices can differ.

[Check the current App Store price](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gitsync-md/id6758960270).

## Android

GitSync.md is free to download from Google Play with a one-time Full Access in-app purchase. There is no subscription. Check the Google Play listing and in-app purchase screen for the current local price and included features. iPhone guide coverage does not imply identical Android features.

[Open Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bontecou.syncmd).

## Optional services and operating-system limits

Background work on iPhone and iPad depends on iOS scheduling, network availability, and Git safety checks. Purchase does not guarantee continuous background synchronization. Git hosting and other third-party services can have their own terms and charges.

For support or purchase questions, see [Contact](https://gitsync.md/contact). Read the [privacy policy](https://gitsync.md/privacy) and [terms](https://gitsync.md/terms) for the app's data handling and service conditions.
